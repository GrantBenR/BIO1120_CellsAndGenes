### Key Questions
- What are the basic features that characterize something as being alive?
- What are the chemical similarities between living and non-living things?
- What is the scientific process in the context of biology?

### Levels of Organization
- Living things are highly organized and structured. Follow a hierarchy on a scale from small to large.

### The Diversity of Life
- There are 3-100 million different species on our planet. 
- 1.7 million are named. 
- 13000 new species are added every year.
- All use the same kind of DNA, same 4 nucleotides, etc.

### Three Domains
- Eukarya: Protists, Plants, Fungi, Animals.
- Archaea: Single-celled prokaryotes that live in harsh environments.
- Bacteria: Widespread diverse group of single-celled prokaryotes.

### Theodosius Dobzhansky (1900-1975)
- Immigrated from Ukraine in 1927.
- Widely considered to be the "father of population genetics".
- Reconciled God and the science through the idea that "the Creator brought about his plan through the process of evolution".
- "Nothing in Biology makes sense except in the light of evolution."

### The Nature of Science
- The world operates on a set of natural and often mathematical laws.
- Science aims to understand the natural world

### The Scientific Method
- Sir Francis Bacon (1561-1626)
- Observation
- Hypothesis Formation
- Prediction
- Experimentation
- Conclusion
- Bacon argued that truth required evidence from the real world (not just arguing by clever people).
### Hypothesis
- Must be testable to ensure its validity.
- Musy be falsifiable (can be disproven).
### Experiment
- Tests the hypothesis.
- Must be designed to test only one variable at a time.
### Basic and Applied Science
- Basic Science: Seeks to expand knowledge regardless of the short-term application of that knowledge.
- Applied Science: Aims to use science to solve real-world problems.
