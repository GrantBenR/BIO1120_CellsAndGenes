### Mating in yeast

### Multi-cellular communications
- Gap junctions - holes in cell walls that allow molecules to travel back and forth between cells
### Local signaling
- one cell releases a bunch of molecules to send a signal to nearby cells.
### Reception of signals
1. Receptor
    - signalling molecule bonds to receptor protein in phospholipid bilayer of receiving cell
2. Transduction
    - signal is transformed or relayed to become activateable
3. Response
    - activation of cellular response

### Receptor Types
- G protein coupled receptors
- Tyrosine kinase receptors
    - accepts and phosphorolates ligand signal molecules
- Ion channel receptors
    - when signal molecule is received, ion gate is opened and ions are channeled into the cell
### Allosterism
- When a molecule changes shape or function as a response
### Intracellular Receptors
- testosterone is hydrophobic steroid, can pass through phospholipid bilayer without channel
- attaches to hormone receptor protein which links to DNA in nucleus to active or deactivate genes
