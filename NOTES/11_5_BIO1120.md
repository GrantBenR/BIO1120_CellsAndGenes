### Hershey Chase
- Set out to prove that DNA is the transforming agent/genetic material

### Chargaff's Rule
- No matter what organism you look at, the molar ratios were always the same for A-G, T-C, A-T, G-C, purines/pyrimidines

### Nucleotides
- Each nucleotide is made of a phosphate group, a sugar, nad a nitrogenous base
- The four types of nitrogenous bases in DNA are Guanine (G), Adenine (A), Cytosine (C), nad Thymine (T).
- Guanine and adenine are purines and cytosine and thymine are pyrimidines

### Rosalind Franklin's X-ray diffraction
- Crystals of DNA give rise to a pattern that is rooted in the position of atoms in DNA molecules
- Rosalind Franklin was able to glean measurements of three repeating features in DNA molecules.

### Base Pairing
- Adenine pairs iwth thymine and cytosine pairs with guanine

### The double helix
- Each DNA molecule is made up of two strands twisted around each other to form a double helix
- Each strand is made long strings of nucleotide monomers
- These strands are held together by hydrogen bonds
- The strands are anti-parallel. One strand has the 3' carbon of the sugar and other other strand will have the 5' carbon of the phosphate in the "upward" position.

### Molecular Biology
- The study of life at a molecular level. Most molecular biologists focus on the information in DNA and the roles of RNA.
