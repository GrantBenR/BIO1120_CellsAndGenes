### Hardy Weinberg equilibrium
- Gene frequencies will only change if one or more ofthe following is not true
    - no mutation
    - random mating
    - no migration
    - no selection
    - infinite population size

### Thomas Hunt Morgan
- Used fruit flies rather than Mendel's peas
- Reproduce in a week rather than 6 months
