### Errors in Meiosis
- Chromosomes disorders can be of two types:
    - Abnormalities in chromosome number (caused by non-disjunction)
        - Duplication (Trisomy)
            - Example: Down's Syndrome
        - Deletion (monosomy)
        - Changes in the number of complete sets of chromosomes
    - Chromosome structural rearrangements
        - Inversion
            - a bit of an allele is partially inverted/flipped compared to its partner
        - Translocation
            - alleles get mismatched
            - typically only discovered due to a fertility problem

### Mendel's experiments
- Genetics is the study of heredity
- Gregor Mendel Ascertained how like begets like
- He experimented with garden peas and kept detailed records of how traits passed from one generation to the next
- Augustinian monk

### Why Peas?
- It naturally self-fertilizes
- Flower petals remain sealed tightly until pollination is completed
    - Results in "true-breeding" pea plants (and many true-breeding varieties were available to him)
    - No unexpected traits in offspring occur
- Short generation times
- easy to grow, low cost of upkeep
- Easy to get large numbers of progeny
- No ethical constraints on mating experiments

### Mendel's first mating experiment
- Mendel mated two contrasting, true-breeding varieties
- True breeding parents are called the P(arental) generation
- Hybrid offspring of the P generation are called F1 (first filial) generation
- When F1 generation self-pollinate, the F2 generation is produced

### Mendel's conclusions
- When Mendel crossed violet flowers with white, all the offspring were violet (F1 generation)
- Crossing F1 generation produced both violet and white flowers in a 3:1 (705:224) ratio (F2 generation)
- Mendel concluded that characteristics could be divided into expressed and latent traits (dominant and recessive)
    - Dominant traits are those that are inherited unchanged in a hybridization
    - Recessive traits become latent, or invisible (but unchaged) in the offspring of a hybridization
