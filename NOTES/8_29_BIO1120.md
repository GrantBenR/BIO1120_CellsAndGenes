### Learning Objectives
- Discuss the unique chemical and physical properties of water and how they facilitate processes essential for life
- Determine the concentration of solutes (including H+ ions) within solvents

### Water has the highest density of polar covalent bonds of any molecule

### Properties of Water
- Polar bonds
- Cohesion (H bonding between H20 molecules)
    - Tendency of water molecules to stick together (surface tension)
- Adhesion (H bonding between H2O and other substances)
    - Helps in transport: capillary action, transpiration
- Temperature Regulation
- Good Solvent
    - Due to its polarity, it is good at pulling in ions of positive or negative charge
- When water temperature decreases, density does not increase.

### specific heat
- The amount of heat needed to raise the temperature of 1 gram of substance by 1 degree celcius

### Avogadro's number
- 6 * 10^23 molecules in one gram

### p
- p = log_10(10^-7)
