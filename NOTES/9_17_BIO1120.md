### Cell Theory
- All living things are made up of cells
- Cells are the smallest living unit of structure and function of all living organisms.
- All cells arise from pre-existing cells.
- <b>Microscopy</b>: Cells vary in size and most cells are microscopic.

### Louis Pasteur (1822 - 1895)
- 1861 experiment disproved the theory of spontaneous generation
- Mice migrated, rags never turned into mice.

### Cell size
- Prokaryotic cell size range: 0.1 - 5.0 micrometers
- Eukaryotic cell size range: 10 - 100 micrometers

### Microscopes
- Light microscope
    - light passes through lenses to magnify an image up to 400x
    - Oil immersion lenses increase magnification up to 1000x
    - Produces an inverted image
- Dissecting Microscope
    - Magnification is 20-80x the object size
    - Provides a sense of depth
    - Shows the image the right side up
- Electron Microscope
    - Scanning Electron Microscope: shows cell's surface characteristics
    - Transmission Electron Microscoe: Shows cell's internal structure

### Prokaryotes vs Eukaryotes
- Prokaryotes have no nucleus or internal membrane
- "Pro" - Without
- "Eu" - With
- Prokaryote Components:
    - Nucleoid
    - Ribosomes
    - Capsule (Only in some)
    - Flagellum
    - Cell Wall (In some Eukaryotes)
    - Cell Membrane
- Eukaryote Components:
    - Membrane-encolsed nucleus
    - Nucleoulus
    - Mitochondrion
    - Ribosomes
    - Cell Membrane
- Prokaryotes do not have membrane bound organelles

### Prokaryotes vs Eukaryotes Continued
- Prokaryotes Attributes
    - Simple
    - Single Celled
    - Lacks Nucleus
    - Lacks membrane-bound organelles
    - Examples: Bacteria, Archaea
- Eukaryotes Attributes
    - Complicated
    - Has membrane-bound nucleus
    - Has membrane-bound organelles
    - Examples: Plants, pets, and people

### Plasma membrane/Cytoplasm
- Plasma Membrane
    - double layer of phospholipids & proteins
    - Selectively permeable - regulates the passage of some substances
- Cytoplasm
    - Semi-fluid material in which cell organelles "float"
    - Many metabolic reactions, including protein synthesis, take place in the cytoplasm
