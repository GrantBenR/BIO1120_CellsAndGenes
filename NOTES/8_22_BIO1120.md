# 8_22 (Fri) - Biology is the Study of Life

### BASIC CHARACTERISTICS OF LIFE
- Organization/boundaries
- Ability to reproduce
- Ability to respond to stimuli
- Ability to repair damage

### COMMON CHARACTERISTICS OF LIVING THINGS
- <u>Order</u>
- <u>Reproduction</u>
- <u>Response to stimuli</u>
- Homeostasis (including <u>repair damage</u>)
- Growth and development
- Regulation
- Adaptation
- Energy Processing
