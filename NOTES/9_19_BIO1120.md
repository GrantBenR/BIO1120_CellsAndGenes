### Reticuloendothelial system
- A group of membranes and organelles in eukaryotic cells that work together to modify, package and transport lipids and proteins. Sometimes called the "endomembrane system." It includes:
    - Nuclear Envelope
    - Lysosomes
    - Vesicles
    - Endoplasmic reticulum
    - Golgi apparatus
### The nucleus and its parts
- Nucleus
    - Houses the cell's DNA in the form of chromatin
    - Directs the synthesis of ribosomes and proteins.
- Part of the nucleus
    - Nuclear envelope (double membrane)
    - Chromatin (combination of DNA and proteins)
    - Nucleolus (site of ribosome assembly)
    - Histone (protein associated with DNA)
### Endoplasmic reticulum
- Network of interconnected membranes
- Helps move substances within cells
- Two types:
    - <b>Rough endoplasmic reticulum:</b>
        - Ribosomes are attached to its cytoplasmic surface
        - Synthesizes and modifies proteins
    - <b>Smooth endpoplasmic reticulum:</b>
        - No ribosomes on its cytoplasmic surface
        - Synthesizes lipids
### Golgi apparatus
- Sorts, tags, packages, and distributes lipids and proteins
- Modified and tagged proteins are packaged into vesicles
    - Transport vesicles: Deposit their contents into other parts of the cell where they will be used
    - Secretory vesicles: Fuse with the plasma membrane and release their contents outside the cell.
- In plant cells, the Golgi apparatus synthesizes polysaccharides
### Lysosomes
- Contains digestive enzymes
- Functions:
    - Breaks down old cell organelles
    - Aids in cell renewal
    - Destroys disease-causing organisms (phagocytosis)
### Vesicles and Vacuoles
- Membrane bound sacs that function in storage and trasport
- Vacuoles:
    - Larger than vesicles
    - Membrane does not fuse with membranes of other cellular components
- Vesicles:
    - Can fuse with other membranes within cells.
### Other parts
- Ribosomes
    - protein synthesis
- Centrioles
    - Made from microtubules
    - Anchors for mitotic/meiotic spindles
- Peroxisomes
    - Oxidizes and breaks down fatty acids and amino acids, and detoxifies poisons.
### Chloroplasts (in plant cells)
- Derived from photosynthetic bacteria (endosymbiont theory)
- Have their own DNA and ribosomes
- Contain chlorophyll for photosynthesis
- Componenents:
    - intermembrane space
    - outer membrane
    - inner membrane
    - Granum (stack of thylakoids)
    - Stroma (aqeous fluid)
### Mitochondria
- Derived from free-living bacteria (endosymbiont theory)
- Have their own DNA and ribosomes
- Contain internal membranes for electron transport chain
