### Overview of photosynthesis
- Photosynthesis is the process through which organisms convert solar energy into chemical energy.
- This chemical energy is used to build glucose and other carbohydrate molecules
- The energy released when these carbohydrate molecules are broken down is used ot perform work, such as cellular respiration
- Photosynthesis also releases ozygen into the atmosphere.
### Trophy
- Role of Solar Energy in Good Production
- Plants and certain types of bacteria and algae use solar energy to make food
    - Autotrophs: produce their own food
    - Photoautotrophs: use photosynthesis to convert photo-electric energy into chemical energy stored in carbohydrates
    - Chemoautotrophs: use energy-rich molecules like H2S to synthesize carbohydrates
    - Heterotrophs:
        - Cannot carry out the process of photosynthesis
        - They obtain energy by consuming other ogranisms/autotrophs.
### Photosynthetic plant structures
- Chloroplasts: organelles within hte mesophyll where photosynthesis takes place
- Thylakoid: disc scahped structure within the chloroplast
- Granum: stack of thylakoids
- Stroma: space surrounding the granum
- Chlorophyll: A pigment that absorbs light embedded in the thylakoid membrane
- Mesophyll: middle layer of the leaf where photosynthesis occurs
- Stomata: Gas exchange of O2 and CO2

### light energy
- Photons have a dual nature: both particles and waves
- A wave's energy is related to its wavelength
- Each wavelength corresponds to a different amount of energy carried.
- The energy carried is inversely proportional to the wavelength
